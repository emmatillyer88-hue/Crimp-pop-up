A pop up pork pie shop for Christmas!

@ Northdown Butchers.
Friday 18th and Saturday 19th December.

Hand raised pork pies made to a traditional recipe using locally grown and milled Kentish flour from Nonington farms and pork from Northdown butchers. Northdown is a whole-carcass, nose-to-tail butcher shop using native breed pigs from Snoad farm near Faversham and Wheatsheaf farm near Stockbury.

Perfect for Christmas Eve parties or the Boxing Day buffet table. Available in shop from Friday 18th December until sold out.

Beat the rush and preorder!

The pies will be wrapped in unbleached waxed paper, tied with natural twine and finished with a Crimp logo sticker, perfect for gifting as well as eating.

The Traditional

Pork, leek and white pepper

6 inch 1kg pork pie

The traditional pie is made with crisp lard pastry and a pate of diced and minced pork shoulder and belly, bacon, leek, white pepper, herbs, spices and finished with a rich pork jelly.

Ingredients: Pork, pork fat, bacon trim, leek, garlic, butter, rosemary, thyme, black pepper, white pepper, salt, flour, lard, egg, pork bone, trotter, chicken, onion, carrot, parsley, gelatin.

About Crimp

Hi I’m Emma, the baker and pastry chef behind Crimp, a pop up supper club and pie shop based in Margate. You’ll currently find me slinging baguettes at Oast bakery but before this I was living and working in London and Manchester honing my pastry craft at some amazing places including Where the Light Gets In and Flor to name a few. I’m passionate about seasonality and local produce, and pies of course….the true bridge between the sweet and savoury kitchen and one of life’s great pleasures!
